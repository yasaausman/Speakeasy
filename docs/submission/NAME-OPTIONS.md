# Name options

**Decision (2026-09-13): the product is now named KindlyCall.** The rename has been applied across the app, code, and docs. The exploration below is kept for the record; note the earlier web check flagged an existing "KindlyCall" automated check-in service (see "Names screened out"), which the team accepted when choosing this name.

## Strongest directions

| Candidate | Pronunciation / feel | Why it could work | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bolara** | boh-LAH-rah; warm, concise | A coined product identity inspired by “bolo”; easy to give the orb this personality. Broad enough for more languages and tasks. | Needs the descriptor “Calls for you.” Existing place names and other unrelated uses appeared in search. |
| **OnBehalf** | on-beh-HALF; capable, reassuring | Clearly expresses delegation and user agency. Strong across appointments, follow-ups and errands. | More formal and less immediately familiar to some non-English speakers. |
| **SayRelay** | SAY-ree-lay; light, memorable | Suggests carrying a request across a language barrier. | Could be mistaken for messaging or translation alone. |
| **BoloBuddy** | BOH-loh-buddy; friendly | Strong fit for the current mascot and Hindi demo. | More playful; may sound like language practice rather than a trusted task assistant. |
| **VoyaCall** | VOY-ah-call; modern | Voice/travel associations and explicit phone category. | Could imply a travel-only product. |
| **CallMitra** | call-MIT-rah; companion | Signals a helpful calling companion and fits an Indian-language introduction. | More regional brand identity; pronunciation should be tested. |
| **ApniCall** | UP-nee-call; personal | Can support a “your call, your language” positioning. | Less intuitive to audiences unfamiliar with Hindi. |
| **CallAmi** | call-AH-mee; soft, compact | Phone category with a personable sound, easy to use as a mascot name. | Needs a clear descriptor; check pronunciation across languages. |
| **Bolayo** | boh-LAH-yoh; expressive | Short and speech-associated as a creative direction. | Existing surname/company/word uses appeared in search; not an exclusive coinage. |

Recommendation: **Bolara** for a distinct consumer identity, **OnBehalf** for immediate product meaning, **SayRelay** for the language-bridge story.

## Presentation examples

**Bolara**  
Your words. We make the call.  
Book everyday appointments in your language—even when the business only speaks English.

**OnBehalf**  
A little help on the other end of the line.  
Your AI assistant for appointments and everyday phone tasks.

**SayRelay**  
Ask in your language. Get it done.  
An AI assistant that handles English phone calls for you.

## Names screened out

- **CallSaathi**: existing AI telephone-companionship service, https://www.callsaathi.com/
- **HelloSet**: existing productivity app and brand, https://helloset.com/en/
- **KindlyCall**: existing automated check-in service, https://ai4agedcare.com/
- **BoloBridge**: existing multilingual speech-wellness product, https://bolobridge.org/
- **Speakeasy**: existing conversation/speech products make differentiation harder, including https://speakeasy-app.com/

These were preliminary public-web checks on September 13, 2026. The candidates are not cleared for domains, app-store names or trademarks; no purchase or registration was made. A search returning no obvious app does not prove availability.

## Choose quickly

Ask the Hindi-speaking tester to pronounce the top three, hear each once, and explain what they think it does. Pick the one they remember with the fewest incorrect assumptions. Keep the decision to about 15 minutes before recording. Once chosen, update the visible app name, README, video title and submission consistently; avoid changing bundle IDs or infrastructure at the deadline.
