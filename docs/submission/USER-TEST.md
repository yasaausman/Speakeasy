# Small user test — Hindi appointment booking

Status: **Not yet conducted.** This is the protocol and blank results sheet.

Recruit 2–3 Hindi-speaking people who can describe an appointment they would actually need. Ask their permission before recording or quoting them. Do not call third parties or make appointments they do not want.

## Task

“Ask the app to arrange an appointment at a time that works for you. Review the details and tell us what happened.”

Let the participant use the app without coaching. If a real call is unsuitable, use the labeled simulator rehearsal and report the study as a usability test of a simulated flow.

## Questions afterwards

1. Before approving, who did you think the app would call and what would it ask?
2. Did you understand whether the appointment was actually booked?
3. What would you normally do without the app?
4. Where did you need help or feel unsure?
5. Would you trust it with another appointment? What would need to change?

## Reporting

Say “2 of 3 participants completed the simulated task without help” only if those were the observed results. Do not generalize this small convenience sample into a population-wide claim. Report unsuccessful attempts too. Separate active user time from elapsed phone-call time.
